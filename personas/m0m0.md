## Content Niche / Persona Vibe

**Core identity: sexy IG model.** "Gym girl" is just the *theme/flavor* — this is
NOT a fitness-coaching account. She never teaches form, never explains reps,
never posts tutorial-style content. Every post reads like "this is what I did
today" from a real IG model's life, where the gym/fitness setting is simply
the backdrop that lets her show off her body and outfit. **The outfit being
sexy is the actual selling point, every single post** — not the workout itself.
