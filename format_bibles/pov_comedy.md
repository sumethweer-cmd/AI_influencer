# Format Bible: POV Comedy

```
Camera:
- Represents another person's viewpoint (the "camera" IS a character in the scene)
- Handheld energy, reacts to what the character does (e.g. flinches, follows her movement)

Body:
- Played toward the "camera-as-person," not toward an audience watching from outside
- Reactions escalate toward the comedic beat

Eyes:
- Direct to lens = looking at the implied other person

Movement:
- Exaggerated (but not overacted) reactions timed to the joke structure's Twist/Punchline

Lighting:
- Matches whatever scene/setting the bit implies

Avoid:
- Breaking the POV conceit (looking away as if performing to a separate audience)
- Underplaying the comedic beat into flatness
```

## Best Suited For
`comedy.md` specifically — this format only makes sense paired with a joke structure.
