# Format Bible: CCTV / Security Cam

Source: adapted from an external smartphone-aesthetic prompt library
(camera/scene mechanics rewritten in this project's own schema and voice —
no text copied verbatim; NSFW-permission and output-length rules from the
source were dropped, since `constraints.md` and `model_specs/*.md` already
own those decisions in this pipeline).

The most extreme "she doesn't know she's being filmed" premise in this
folder — even more detached than `candid_single_take.md`, since the
"camera" here isn't a phone she's aware of at all, it's a fixed ceiling
unit she has no reason to think about.

```
Camera:
- Fixed, high, wide-angle from a ceiling corner looking down — never at
  eye level, never handheld
- Mild fisheye/barrel distortion at the frame edges; soft focus toward
  the corners, sharper toward the center
- Static the entire shot — this format has zero camera movement, ever

Framing/Image quality:
- Low-resolution, grainy, washed-out picture; low-bitrate blocky
  compression; faint interlacing/scanline texture; low frame-rate motion
  carries a slight stutter/blur on movement
- A small on-screen timestamp + camera-ID overlay in one corner (e.g.
  "2024-06-12 03:47:21 CAM 02") sells the format instantly — include it

Body:
- Completely unaware of being observed — natural, private, unguarded
  behavior with zero performance quality
- Ordinary small actions (checking a phone, adjusting something, walking
  through frame) read better than anything posed

Eyes:
- Never toward the camera — there is no "lens" she could look at, the
  camera is a ceiling fixture in a corner she isn't oriented toward

Lighting:
- Flat overhead surveillance lighting — harsh ceiling fluorescent with
  blown highlights and crushed shadows is the daytime look; a green-tinted
  infrared night-vision glow is the low-light alternative

Avoid:
- Any hint of posing or awareness of the camera — this instantly breaks
  the format's entire premise
- Clean, high-resolution rendering — the degraded image quality (grain,
  compression, timestamp overlay) is load-bearing, not a style garnish
```

## Best Suited For
A distinct "found footage" aesthetic beat, not everyday content — use
sparingly as a genre/novelty format (e.g. a "caught on camera" reveal),
never as the default look for regular posting.
