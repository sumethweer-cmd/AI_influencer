# Format Bible: Tripod Outfit Review

```
Camera:
- Fixed tripod, full-body
- May include a slow push-in or the character stepping closer mid-clip

Body:
- Turn/pose sequence — front, side, back/twirl — to show the outfit off
- Confident, showcase-y movement, more deliberate than tripod_casual's talking energy

Eyes:
- Alternates between camera and glancing down at outfit/self

Movement:
- Turning, hem/fabric adjustment, hair touch
- Outfit reveal beat (e.g. jacket open, turn to reveal back)

Lighting:
- Bright enough to actually show outfit detail/color accurately

Avoid:
- Static single-angle standing shot with no turn/movement (defeats the point of a review)
```

## Common Failure Patterns
- Reads as a plain standing portrait instead of a review — must include at
  least one turn/reveal beat in the enhanced_spec's shot list.

## Best Suited For
`ugc.md`, `product_review.md` when the "product" is the outfit itself.
