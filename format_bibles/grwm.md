# Format Bible: GRWM (Get Ready With Me)

```
Camera:
- Tripod or propped phone, bathroom/vanity mirror angle
- Mostly static, occasional handheld cutaway for close-up detail (makeup, product)

Body:
- Task-focused — actually doing the get-ready action (applying makeup, styling hair),
  not posing at camera

Eyes:
- Mostly on the task (mirror/product), brief glances to camera between steps

Movement:
- Hands doing the actual task
- Head tilts to check work in mirror

Lighting:
- Vanity/bathroom lighting, practical and bright enough to see the task clearly

Avoid:
- Looking at camera the whole time instead of doing the task (breaks the GRWM premise)
- Perfectly finished look at "start" of the sequence
```

## Best Suited For
`ugc.md` — inherently a relatable-moment format, works well as a `video_clip`
or `carousel` (before/mid/after beats).
