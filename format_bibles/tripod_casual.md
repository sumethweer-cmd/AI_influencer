# Format Bible: Tripod Casual

```
Camera:
- Fixed tripod, waist-up or full-body, static shot
- Level, not handheld-shaky

Body:
- Natural standing/sitting posture, weight shifts occasionally
- Not posed like a photoshoot — mid-conversation energy

Eyes:
- Direct to camera most of the time (this format IS the "talking to camera" format)

Movement:
- Hand gestures while talking
- Occasional step in/out of frame if the room allows
- Natural blinking, head tilts

Lighting:
- Soft, ambient room lighting — not a ring light unless the character's
  visual_personality.md says she uses one

Avoid:
- Complete stillness for the whole clip
- Reading off a script tone (stiff cadence, no natural pauses)
```

## Common Failure Patterns
- Character feels like she's reciting rather than talking — fix via
  `prompt-enhancer` naturalism notes (pauses, self-interruption, mid-sentence gesture).

## Best Suited For
`opinion`, `fan_interaction`, `storytelling`, `educational` (general
explainer tone, not an intimate/confiding one — see `selfie_cam.md` for
that variant) — general "talking directly to camera" content.
