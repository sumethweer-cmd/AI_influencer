# Format Bible: Car Selfie

Source: adapted from an external smartphone-aesthetic prompt library
(camera/scene mechanics rewritten in this project's own schema and voice —
no text copied verbatim; NSFW-permission and output-length rules from the
source were dropped, since `constraints.md` and `model_specs/*.md` already
own those decisions in this pipeline).

```
Camera:
- Handheld phone, driver's-seat selfie framing — held roughly at chest/chin
  height in her lap or against the wheel
- Seatbelt diagonal crosses the torso in frame; steering wheel and/or
  dashboard visible at the bottom edge; rearview mirror sometimes in shot

Body:
- Relaxed, settled into the seat — not performing for the lens, a "quick
  check before driving off" energy
- Small natural gestures: adjusting the mirror, checking hair/makeup,
  resting a hand on the wheel

Eyes:
- On the lens for a posed beat, but breaks naturally (checking mirror,
  glancing out the windshield) — this isn't a locked stare the whole shot

Lighting:
- Natural daylight through the side window — soft directional light on one
  side of the face is the signature look; golden-hour warm light or harsh
  midday sun through the windshield both read as authentic
- Slight blue-tinted cast from the windshield glass is a natural accent,
  not a heavy color grade

Avoid:
- Studio-clean lighting — the whole point is unmistakably "inside a real
  car," not a car used as a backdrop
- Losing the seatbelt/wheel/dashboard framing cues — without them this is
  indistinguishable from a generic selfie_cam shot
```

## Best Suited For
Casual/lifestyle pillar content — "heading out," "waiting at a red light,"
pre-event mirror-check energy. Distinct from `selfie_cam.md` specifically
because of the car-interior framing cues (seatbelt, wheel, dashboard) —
without those, use `selfie_cam.md` instead.
