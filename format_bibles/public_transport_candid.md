# Format Bible: Public Transport Candid

Source: adapted from an external smartphone-aesthetic prompt library
(camera/scene mechanics rewritten in this project's own schema and voice —
no text copied verbatim; NSFW-permission and output-length rules from the
source were dropped, since `constraints.md` and `model_specs/*.md` already
own those decisions in this pipeline).

```
Camera:
- Handheld phone, either front-camera selfie or held out toward the window/
  aisle — mid-commute, not a set-up shot
- Slight motion blur or a soft vibration reads as the vehicle actually
  moving — never a perfectly stable frame

Setting:
- Metro/train/tram/bus interior: plastic seats, chrome handrails/poles, a
  window with the city or a dark tunnel blurring past, other passengers
  softly out of focus in the background, transit ad panels

Body:
- Low-energy, unposed — resting against the window, earphones in, phone in
  the other hand, minimal deliberate posing
- Mid-commute stillness: not performing, just passing time

Eyes:
- Gaze mostly out the window or down at her own phone — direct-to-lens only
  in brief, casual beats, never a held stare

Lighting:
- Cold white fluorescent carriage light with a faint greenish cast (flat,
  even, slightly unflattering — this is correct for the format, not a
  mistake to fix), or daylight streaming through the window when
  above-ground; flickering light passing through tunnel sections reads as
  an authentic accent, not a flaw

Avoid:
- Warm/flattering "influencer lighting" — undercuts the entire "just
  killing time on the way somewhere" premise
- An empty or deserted carriage — soft out-of-focus other passengers and
  ambient transit life are part of what sells the candid read
```

## Best Suited For
Lifestyle/day-in-the-life pillar content, low-key "in-between" moments —
pairs well with `candid_activity_glimpse.md`'s unposed sensibility but for
a commute/transit setting specifically rather than a home activity.
