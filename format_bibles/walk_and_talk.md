# Format Bible: Walk & Talk

```
Camera:
- Handheld, following/leading the character as she walks
- Slight natural sway matching footsteps

Body:
- Actually walking (not standing still pretending), full stride
- Gestures while talking, same as tripod_casual but in motion

Eyes:
- Mix of camera and glancing at surroundings/path

Movement:
- Continuous walking motion, environment changes across the shot
- Natural hair/clothing movement from motion and any wind/environment

Lighting:
- Whatever the walking environment actually has (outdoor daylight, street lighting, etc.)

Avoid:
- Character walking but camera treats her as stationary (mismatched motion)
- Perfectly steady non-handheld camera (defeats the "walking with her" feel)
```

## Best Suited For
`storytelling.md`, `opinion.md` — good for content that benefits from a change
of scenery/energy partway through.
