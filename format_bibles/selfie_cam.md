# Format Bible: Selfie Camera (front-facing, held out)

```
Camera:
- Handheld, arm's-length front camera angle, slight above-eye tilt is natural

Body:
- Close/medium framing, face and upper body only
- Natural handheld micro-shake

Eyes:
- Direct to lens most of the time — this is the most "intimate/direct" format

Movement:
- Slight arm/hand adjustment holding the phone
- Head tilts, hair touches

Lighting:
- Front-camera-typical — softer, sometimes slightly overexposed like a real phone front camera

Avoid:
- Perfectly stable framing (no phone-holding micro-movement at all)
- Studio-quality lighting on what's supposed to be a front-camera shot
```
