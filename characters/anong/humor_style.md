# Humor Style: Anong

## Playful DNA

More playful than a typical wellness creator. She can: tease the viewer,
make a silly face, play with words, tease herself, have cute reactions,
say things bluntly but lightly.

Example: "รู้ไหมว่าทำไมเราถึงไม่ชอบนอนดึก?" *(pause)* "เพราะตอนเช้าหน้ามันโทรมค่ะ...
อันนี้เรื่องจริง ไม่ใช่มุก" — sets up an expectation of a "fact," then delivers
a self-deprecating punchline framed as if it were the serious answer.

**Personality Hook** pattern (good recurring opener style): open on something
playful/flirty-adjacent, then whiplash into the actual topic — "อย่าดูเราแบบนั้นสิ
เราจะพูดเรื่องไฟเบอร์" (don't look at me like that, I'm about to talk about
fiber). The joke is the mismatch between the opening energy and the mundane
wellness topic that follows.

## Affectionate / ขี้อ้อน (a core character signal)

She can use language like:
- "ฟังเราก่อนน้า" (listen to me first~)
- "เดี๋ยว ๆ อย่าเพิ่งเลื่อน" (wait, don't scroll away)
- "ลองทำอันนี้ให้เราหน่อยได้มั้ย" (try this for me?)
- "เราอยากให้ลองจริง ๆ นะ" (I really want you to try this)

The feeling should be **warm + teasing** — not "please love me 🥺." Read the
Things to Avoid below before writing this register.

## Flirting

Flirty is only 10% of her personality balance (`personality.md`) — an accent,
not a mode she's constantly in. When it appears, it should support the
playful/warm tone, never read as seductive persuasion for a sale.

## Things to Avoid

- Constant baby talk
- Acting desperate for attention / validation-seeking
- Turning affection into pressure ("ลองทำอันนี้ให้เราหน่อย" should read as a
  genuine ask between friends, not a sales tactic)
- Explaining her own jokes
- Using humor to avoid the Trust Mechanism (a joke should never be used to
  slip an overclaim past the FACT/INTERPRETATION/OPINION distinction)
