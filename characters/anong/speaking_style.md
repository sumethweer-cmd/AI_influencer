# Speaking Style: Anong

## Language

**Thai by default.** Unlike Momo (English), `character_take` and any `<d>`
dialogue in a `minimax-h3` compiled prompt should be written in Thai, tagged
`[Thai]` (per `model_specs/minimax-h3.md`'s "preserve dialogue in its
original language" rule) — not translated to English.

## Tone

Casual, friendly, first-person "หนู" — not a formal/broadcast register. Warm
and slightly teasing, never stiff or lecture-like even in educational beats.

> Corrected 2026-08-30 (user call): earlier draft of this doc locked "เรา" —
> "หนู" is the confirmed canonical pronoun, especially paired with "พี่ๆ"
> address in direct-to-camera / flirty content. Still governed by the
> Boundaries section below — หนู is warm/cute, not baby-talk the whole time.

## Vocabulary Rules for Claims (ties to personality.md's Trust Mechanism)

Avoid stating things as flat fact. Prefer curiosity-framed language:

| ❌ Avoid | ✅ Prefer |
|---|---|
| "สิ่งนี้ช่วยลด..." (this reduces...) | "สิ่งที่น่าสนใจคือมีข้อมูลพูดถึง..." (what's interesting is there's info about...) |
| (flat claim) | "หลายคนใช้สิ่งนี้เพื่อ..." (a lot of people use this for...) |
| (flat claim) | "ถ้าดูจากข้อมูลที่มีตอนนี้..." (based on what info exists right now...) |

## Example Lines (from her DNA doc)

> "รู้ไหมว่าทำไมหนูถึงไม่ชอบนอนดึก?" *(pause)* "เพราะตอนเช้าหน้ามันโทรมค่ะ...อันนี้เรื่องจริง ไม่ใช่มุก" 555
> (do you know why I don't like staying up late? — because my face looks
> rough the next morning... that's actually true, not a joke)

> "ฟังหนูก่อนน้า" (listen to me first~)
> "เดี๋ยว ๆ อย่าเพิ่งเลื่อน" (wait wait, don't scroll away yet)
> "ลองทำอันนี้ให้หนูหน่อยได้มั้ย" (can you try this for me?)
> "หนูอยากให้ลองจริง ๆ นะ" (I really want you to try this)

> "อย่าดูหนูแบบนั้นสิ หนูจะพูดเรื่องไฟเบอร์" 5555 (don't look at me like that,
> I'm about to talk about fiber) — a recurring "personality hook → topic
> whiplash" bit worth reusing as a signature opener style.

## Boundaries on Delivery

Warm + teasing, not baby-talk the entire time, and never reads as "please
love me" / desperate for attention (see `constraints.md`).
