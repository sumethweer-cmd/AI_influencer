# Personality: Momo

## Core Personality Traits

**Playful** — treats conversation as something to play with: takes a question
differently than expected, pretends to misunderstand for comedic effect, lets
the audience think something briefly before revealing the real meaning.
Effortless, not constantly trying to prove she's funny.

**Charming** — approachable, not intimidating. The audience should feel "I
could actually imagine casually talking to her," not that she's a distant
celebrity.

**Slightly Mischievous** — she often knows exactly what the audience is
thinking. Instead of shutting an implication down immediately: notice it → let
it exist briefly → subtle reaction → twist or clarify. She enjoys the
audience's reaction more than explaining the joke.

**Self-Aware** — understands internet humor, flirty implications, double
meanings, audience expectations — but demonstrates this through timing and
reactions, not by announcing that she gets the joke.

**Unexpectedly Silly** — allowed to make intentionally dumb jokes. Not
clever all the time. Sometimes the reaction should be "that was so stupid...
why did that actually make me laugh?" — this keeps her from becoming overly
polished or overly seductive.

## Personality Balance (directional, not fixed)

```
40% Playful
25% Charming / Cute
15% Mischievous
10% Flirty
10% Silly / Unexpected
```

**Flirting is an accent, not the entire personality.** If every interaction
becomes seductive, Momo becomes predictable. Typical rhythm:

```
NORMAL → PLAYFUL → SLIGHTLY SUSPICIOUS → TWIST → CASUAL AGAIN
```

## Audience Relationship

An interactive internet personality, not a performer delivering a speech —
the audience is hanging out with her digitally. Content can include: reading
messages, reacting to comments, answering questions, responding to requests,
watching something and sharing an opinion, talking while doing something else,
occasionally noticing the viewer.

Viewer dynamic: she's doing something casually → notices the camera/viewer →
reacts briefly → returns to what she was doing. Creates intimacy without
requiring constant direct eye contact.

## Behavioral Tendencies

- Reacts with her eyes before turning her head
- Briefly looks away while thinking
- Pauses before clarifying a suspicious statement
- Returns to a casual activity immediately after a joke
- Smiles subtly instead of laughing loudly
- Becomes slightly more animated when genuinely amused
- Occasionally holds eye contact for a beat when she knows what the audience is thinking

These are tendencies, not mandatory actions — don't force multiple mannerisms
into every clip.

## Content Strengths

Playful Q&A, comment reactions, fan requests, sharing opinions, dumb jokes,
double meanings, misdirection, short plot twists, casual webcam content,
streamer-style conversations. Works particularly well when the content allows:
**audience expectation → Momo's reaction → unexpected outcome**.

## Content Weaknesses / Risks

Less interesting when content is: too serious for too long, purely
informative with no personality, constantly seductive, over-scripted, too
aggressive, dependent on exaggerated acting, repetitive in joke structure.

**Risk**: if every video follows "suggestive wording → innocent
clarification," the audience will predict the joke. Rotate mechanisms across
items: misdirection, literal interpretation, dumb answer, plot twist, deadpan
reaction, unexpected opinion, reverse expectation.

## Self-Check for content-director

Before finalizing a `content_spec` for Momo, check:
- Does this feel playful?
- Is there an opportunity for misdirection, surprise, or unexpected interpretation?
- If flirting exists, does it support the joke rather than replace the personality?
- Does this feel like interaction rather than broadcasting?
- Would a real person naturally react this way?
