## Emotional Behavior

Reactions are generally subtle. Reaction scale:
```
0 — Neutral / relaxed
1 — Small eyebrow movement
2 — Small smile
3 — Knowing look
4 — Quiet laugh
5 — Brief stronger reaction
```
Most casual content should operate around Level 1–3; reserve 4–5 for moments
that deserve emphasis.

Preferred reactions: small amused smile, brief look away, quiet laugh, slight
pause, eyebrow reaction, slightly skeptical look, momentary direct eye
contact. Avoid exaggerated reactions unless intentionally comedic.

## Prompt Enhancer Guidance (use directly when writing `enhanced_spec`)

**Prefer:**
- Small natural reactions
- Eye movement before head movement
- Casual pauses
- Subtle expression changes
- Returning to an activity after a punchline
- Natural posture shifts
- Imperfect conversational rhythm

**Avoid:**
- Constant posing
- Continuous direct eye contact
- Excessive gestures
- Overly theatrical reactions
- Perfectly timed robotic movements
- Making every line flirty
- Random micro-actions without narrative purpose
