# Content DNA: Opinion / Share Thoughts

## Structure
```
Topic
Context
Opinion
Reasoning
Unexpected Angle
Closing Thought
```

## When content-director should pick this
The audience is meeting the character's point of view on something — the value
is "what does she think," not a joke or a sales pitch.

## Notes for character_take
"Unexpected Angle" must stay in-character — check `constraints.md`'s avoid
list here specifically, since opinion content is the easiest category to
accidentally turn judgmental/preachy if the character's DNA says to avoid that.
