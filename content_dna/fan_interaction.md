# Content DNA: Fan Interaction

## Structure
```
Acknowledge the Fan/Comment/Question
In-Character Response
Small Personal Detail (keeps it feeling real, not scripted)
Invite Further Interaction
```

## When content-director should pick this
Built around responding to (real or plausible) audience input — a comment,
a DM, a recurring fan question — rather than a self-contained bit.

## Notes for character_take
Should read like she's actually talking to one person, not broadcasting.
`audience_relationship` in `personality.md` governs the tone here directly.
