# Content DNA: Reaction / Commentary

## Structure
```
Source Clip/Topic Intro
Initial Reaction
In-Character Take (the character_take line)
Elaboration / Riff
Sign-off
```

## When content-director should pick this
The piece is her reacting to something external (a trend, a clip, a topic) —
this is the "streamer reaction" mechanic. The point is her personality's take
on it, not a verdict on whether the source content is good or bad.

## Notes for character_take
This is the category from the worked example: a neutral/generic source topic
goes in, and what comes out is a playful, in-character line — never a
judgmental critique ("that's not right because...") unless the character's
humor_style.md explicitly does that as a bit. Default to teasing/amused, not
moralizing.
