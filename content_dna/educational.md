# Content DNA: Educational

## Structure
```
Hook (a myth, a question, or a problem)
Explanation / Reality (what's actually going on)
Practical Takeaway
```
Alternate shape when framed as myth-busting:
```
Myth
↓
Reality
```

## When content-director should pick this
The point of the piece is to explain something — the audience should walk
away knowing something they didn't before. Distinct from `opinion.md` (the
value there is the character's point of view) and from `product_review.md`/
`affiliate.md` (those center a specific product, not a general topic).

## Notes for character_take
Keep the character positioned as a curator/enthusiast explaining something
interesting, not a credentialed authority, unless a specific character's DNA
explicitly grants that authority. Watch for overclaiming — a character whose
`personality.md`/`constraints.md` defines a fact/interpretation/opinion
distinction (e.g. Anong) must keep those layers separate in this category
especially, since educational content is the easiest place to accidentally
state a claim more strongly than it's actually supported.
