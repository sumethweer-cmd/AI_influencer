# Content DNA: Storytelling

## Structure
```
Hook (in medias res, or a question)
Setup
Rising Detail
Turning Point
Resolution
Takeaway / Feeling Left With
```

## When content-director should pick this
A self-contained narrative — something happened (real or in-persona-fiction),
and the value is the story itself, not a joke, opinion, or pitch.

## Notes for character_take
Best suited to `delivery_format: carousel` or `video_clip` where the story can
unfold across multiple shots/slides with continuity — check with
delivery-format-selector before assuming a single image can carry it.
