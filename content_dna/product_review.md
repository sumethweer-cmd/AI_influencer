# Content DNA: Product Review

## Structure
```
Hook (the claim or question)
First Impression
Testing / Using It
Verdict
Who It's For
```

## When content-director should pick this
A specific product/service is the subject and the audience expects an honest
(in-character) take on it — distinct from UGC, where the product is secondary
to a relatable moment.

## Notes for character_take
The verdict must stay in the character's voice (per `speaking_style.md`) —
avoid generic reviewer language ("10/10 would recommend") unless that's
actually her style.
